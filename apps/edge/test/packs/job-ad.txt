Customer Assistant
Company: Fairfield Foods
Location: Leeds, West Yorkshire
Salary: £12.60 per hour
Closing date: 20 November 2026

About the role
We are looking for a friendly Customer Assistant to join our busy Leeds store. You will help customers, keep the shelves full and work on the tills.

Responsibilities:
- Serve customers on the tills and answer their questions
- Keep shelves stocked and tidy
- Follow food safety and health and safety rules

Requirements:
- Previous retail or customer service experience
- Good communication and teamwork
- Ability to lift and carry stock
- A full UK driving licence
- Forklift licence (desirable)

Ghosty's daily GPU: made by Ghosty on its owner's account, used only by Ghosty's brain (every call needs its key).
It uses the free daily ZeroGPU minutes to practise puzzles for the next week's training. Nothing of the owner's is
kept here: the week's data is read from his private repos at call time. A free account cannot buy GPU time.

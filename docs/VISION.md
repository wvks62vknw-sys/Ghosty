# Ghosty — the vision, part by part

What each part of Ghosty is for, in plain words. Started on 31 October 2026 with God's Eye; the other parts are still to
be written here.

## The Eye

God's Eye is his live globe of public feeds — aircraft, buses and trams, ships, satellites, road incidents, cameras —
and the Machine view is its one-tap mode for watching his own city: MACHINE VIEW, first in the Eye's bar (or "machine
view", "show me my city" or "what are you watching?" in the chat, answered with no model), frames his saved home (the
phone's home setting until he saves one, and it says so) from 14 km away at a 50° tilt, turns on what moves and turns
off the clutter while keeping his own layer choices apart, draws thin boxes on his saved places (amber with a pulsing
ring while a flood or weather warning is active there), on the three nearest aircraft with their callsigns and heights
and on the nearest bus or tram with its line, and says in one line the true count of what the phone is tracking within
25 km (satellites only above 10°), any warning near home or "Nothing active near Home", "No ships feed here" where there
is none, and today's first expectation from foresight; it drifts slowly round, pausing while he touches the globe, shows
its reading on the SEE hub's Eye card, and LEAVE gives back the layers and the camera exactly as they were. Everything
in it comes from the public feeds the phone already reads: the vehicles are public vehicles, never people, nothing about
any person is shown or guessed, and the brain never sees his live position.

## The Workbench

The Workbench (MAKE → WORKBENCH, or "how strong is a 3 m steel beam", "what gear ratio…" in the chat) is his engineering
desk: first estimates from Ghosty's own formulae — a beam or a column, gears, belts and chains, an engine's power and
torque, a wing and a car's drag and top speed, a throw and an orbit, a room's heat loss, the sun and a shadow, a circuit —
worked on the phone with no model and no fetch, in metric or imperial, and by the brain's `estimate` tool when he asks in
the chat. Every answer shows its method, what it assumed, what would change it and a confidence word (rough, fair or
good), and every number says FIRST ESTIMATE, NOT CERTIFIED; anything that holds up a load or a life ends "a chartered
engineer signs this, not me", and a question outside what a formula covers says so and gives no number. Its wind tunnel
is a small 2-D flow running in the phone's shared worker (a lattice-Boltzmann solver) round a wing section, a cylinder, a
plate, a car's outline or a shape he draws with a finger, with live lift and drag coefficients and the Reynolds number
they stand for: a picture of how air behaves at low speed in two dimensions, for learning and comparing, never a number
to build to. Nothing on it can bill him, and nothing leaves the phone.

## Knowledge

When he wants facts he can rely on — CITED on, "with sources", "is it true…", "did … really", "what's the evidence", or
any question about a contested public matter (genocide, war crimes, the ICJ and the ICC, the Epstein files, a cover-up,
corruption), which is cited without asking — Ghosty looks it up first and answers with numbered sources, each with its
tier in a word: PRIMARY (the record itself: a court's judgment, a UN body's or a government's own release, the law as
published, official data, a paper), CREDIBLE (careful work with a stated method: Amnesty, Human Rights Watch, B'Tselem,
ICIJ, Bellingcat, OCCRP, ProPublica, Full Fact), PRESS (established news that corrects its mistakes), REFERENCE (an
encyclopaedia: its references are the leads), LEAD (TikTok, X, YouTube, Reddit: where a claim is found, never what proves
it) or UNKNOWN. Every claim carries its label in words — established fact, documented allegation, legal finding in
progress, contested, rumour or debunked — and the code, not the model, caps it: a lead alone is a rumour at most, an
established fact needs a primary source or two credible or press reports read in that same turn, and a claim nothing read
supports is contested at best. No false balance on what is documented, no manufactured certainty on what is not. When a
model will not engage with a topic, another is asked; if none will, he is told so in one line, with what the sources read
say. Each morning the Digest reads the BBC, arXiv, Hacker News and Wikipedia's current events and, since 31 October, the
primary and credible sources' own feeds (UN News, the ICC, GOV.UK, new UK legislation, Amnesty, Human Rights Watch, ICIJ,
Bellingcat, OCCRP, ProPublica, Full Fact), each passage filed with its tier. Only public sources and his own data: no
private person is ever searched for, guessed about or profiled.

# Ghosty — the vision, part by part

What each part of Ghosty is for, in plain words. Started on 31 October 2026 with God's Eye; the other parts are still to
be written here.

## The Eye

God's Eye is his live globe of public feeds — aircraft, buses and trams, ships, satellites, road incidents, cameras —
and the Machine view is its one-tap mode for watching his own city: MACHINE VIEW, first in the Eye's bar (or "machine
view", "show me my city" or "what are you watching?" in the chat, answered with no model), frames his saved home (the
phone's home setting until he saves one, and it says so) from 14 km away at a 50° tilt, turns on what moves and turns
off the clutter while keeping his own layer choices apart, draws thin boxes on his saved places (amber with a pulsing
ring while a flood or weather warning is active there), on the three nearest aircraft with their callsigns and heights
and on the nearest bus or tram with its line, and says in one line the true count of what the phone is tracking within
25 km (satellites only above 10°), any warning near home or "Nothing active near Home", "No ships feed here" where there
is none, and today's first expectation from foresight; it drifts slowly round, pausing while he touches the globe, shows
its reading on the SEE hub's Eye card, and LEAVE gives back the layers and the camera exactly as they were. Everything
in it comes from the public feeds the phone already reads: the vehicles are public vehicles, never people, nothing about
any person is shown or guessed, and the brain never sees his live position.

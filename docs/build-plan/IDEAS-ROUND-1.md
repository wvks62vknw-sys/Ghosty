# IDEAS ROUND 1 — the owner's ChatGPT brainstorm (4 Nov), triaged against the bar and the means

The owner pasted a long list of ideas from a brainstorm with another assistant (4 Nov, evening). This is the triage: what is
already built (the brainstorm did not know), what to adopt and in what order, what to refuse and why. Sizes are a single
builder's hours plus one checks run; nothing starts before Friday 7 Nov's reset. STANDARD.md remains the bar; PLAN-STARS the
order of passes; this file feeds it.

## Already built (take off the list; say so when it comes up again)

- Verification of facts: six source tiers and six claim labels (established fact … debunked) with the sourcing caps as code,
  carried through cited answers, the search, the library and the Digest.
- Every action recorded: WHY he did that, "what did you do today", the day log; crash records for every brain's load and reply.
- Memory: profiles, timelines, diary, patterns, lessons, a source per fact, forgetting well, search by meaning (on the phone
  when the index is whole), the one shared mind across chat, Brief and Telegram.
- Autonomy: scheduled jobs (hourly, daily, at a time), projects with steps, the Workshop with preview and rollback, self-edit
  with the review link, the upgrade planner, the weekly training with its gate (before and after scored on the same hardware).
- Offline brain: trust by category, the independence meter, Sovereign Mode, ASK THE CLOUD / ASK THE BIG BRAIN explicit and never
  silent, the phone-first path only when the model is warm.
- Talking: first spoken word while the rest is written, interruption with recovery, narration instead of spinners, ten turns
  with no tap, Siri's listen link, notices in turn.
- Workbench: first estimates with method, assumptions, what would change it, confidence, NEVER CERTIFIED; the wind tunnel.
- The catalogue held to the code both ways by a test; NEEDS words ("not set up: the key…") on every item that wants a key.
- A rated chess engine with the coach and the review link; the arcade; the games.

## Adopt, in this order (the owner may reorder)

1. **Measured health on every ability** (Pass 2 of PLAN-STARS, made concrete). WORKING / DEGRADED / NEEDS KEY / OFFLINE /
   BROKEN / NOT IMPLEMENTED per catalogue entry, fed by: the CI specs' last results (published as a small JSON the brain reads),
   a daily self-test job on the brain (each tool's smoke call with stand-in inputs where safe), and the phone's own probes
   (model present, mic, camera). Shown on System and on every ABILITIES row and hub card foot. Size: medium (6–8 h).
2. **Verification on actions.** Every tool result carries VERIFIED (checked independently: a read-back, a second source, a
   calculation re-run), ESTIMATED (assumptions stated), or FAILED (the action did not complete); the chat and the voice say
   it; nothing is ever said done because a model wrote a success line. Size: small to medium (4–6 h), shares code with 1.
3. **Context awareness.** Each screen publishes a one-line "what he is looking at" (the screen, the selected thing, its id)
   that the turn carries in a few words (the prompt guard holds: trimmed from the lowest-value prompt part and the trims
   listed), so "make that wall 30 cm longer", "find me a replacement", "read that one" resolve. Size: medium (6–8 h).
4. **Lens → understand → act.** A photographed timetable, letter, receipt or poster is read (OCR on the phone), extracted
   into dates, amounts, names, checked, and offered to the Agenda / spending / memory with APPROVE per item; nothing added
   without the tap. Size: small to medium (4–6 h).
5. **Workflows by words.** "Every morning check the weather and my calendar and show me anything important" becomes a
   multi-step job he writes out, explains what it needs, runs once as a test with the result shown, and switches on only on
   APPROVE; the Agenda lists it; edit by words. No visual block builder. Size: medium (6–8 h).
6. **Research report mode.** "Research X properly" produces a structured report: questions, evidence with source dates,
   contradictions, unknowns, conclusions, references; saved to the library; follow-ups keep the thread. Built on the cited
   pipeline. Size: medium (6–8 h).
7. **Simulation lab.** Interactive parameter play on the Workbench solvers and the wind tunnel (sliders, live redraw, a
   history of what you tried, an explanation of what moved); circuits, projectiles, heat, orbits first. Size: medium (6–8 h).
8. **Memory 2.0 as a pass.** Duplicate detection, contradiction resolution with the owner's word, confidence and expiry per
   fact, an audit view, export/import already exists. Size: medium (6–8 h). The visual knowledge graph: skipped.
9. **Performance project.** Streamed model replies where the filters allow (sentence-level), fewer model calls per turn,
   lazy-loading of heavy screens, a time-to-first-token measure in the SPEED card. Size: medium to large (10–14 h).
10. **Permissions screen.** Per-tool and per-link permissions, read-only modes for links, token expiry shown, one audit list.
    Size: medium (6–8 h).

Also good and small, to fold into the passes above: recently used and favourite abilities on ABILITIES and the jump bar;
empty-state suggestions (largely done in TABS); adjustable font size; reduced motion (exists); better landscape (TV mode exists).

## Refuse or defer, with the reason

- **Twelve named agents with agent-to-agent talk, benchmarking, disagreement resolution.** Free allowances, the 5,500 prompt
  guard and speed say no; the planner + tool chaining + a verification step (2) give the result with one brain.
- **Full editors: video (CapCut), RAW photo, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Notion, a game studio, a shared 3-D engine with physics
  and a real product library.** Each is a company's whole product; on an iPhone web app with no budget they would be exactly
  the slop the owner hates. Keep narrow, excellent versions (poster, SVG, letters and files, a spreadsheet read and summary,
  the house and the shed in 3-D) and export to the real apps. The "wardrobe drawers" depth principle is right: apply it to
  what exists (the house, the car in 3-D, the shed), one thing at a time.
- **Car identification from photographs or number plates of other cars; parts databases.** Crosses the boundary (no private
  person profiled; plates). His own car: the DVSA history, the 3-D car, repairs as first estimates, public garage listings —
  fine and partly there. Parts data is not free; a maybe later for his own car only.
- **CCTV, Ring and IP camera centres.** Ring has no free official API; a large build for one owner. Later, his own devices
  only, if he asks.
- **Extension marketplace.** One owner; the ability-pages generator (BRIEF-ABILITY-PAGES) is the local version.
- **Evolution engine / "prove better before replace" machinery.** The rule already exists: the training gate for the model,
  CI with the guard for code, the review link for self-edits. No new machinery.
- **Historical event playback in God's Eye.** Nice; earthquakes (USGS) and weather (Open-Meteo archive) are free; satellites
  are not. A small pass later if he wants it.
- **"Paper intelligence across 100 papers."** The library, passages, exact supporting passages and the cited pipeline are
  the core; the report mode (6) covers the rest.
- **Universal task objects moving between screens.** The right idea in a heavier shape; context awareness (3) and the
  projects we have get most of it; a shared task object only if (3) shows the need.

## The principle it got most right

"A feature isn't finished because its main button works." That is STANDARD.md's bar restated; the useful addition is the
measured health status on screen (1), so the difference between exists, works and works reliably is visible to the owner
without asking.

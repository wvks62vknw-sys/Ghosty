# Roadmap — agreed with the owner on 1 November 2026, ~02:10 UK (OWNER-ISSUES 91, 92)

His frame: **sideways** (everything he has reaches everywhere and is found) and **significantly upwards** (each pillar
becomes the most it can be). His rule for anything hands-on: **show me, don't tell me** — a problem in plain words, the
real thing on screen, interactive, the fix on it; numbers only when asked. His rule for finding things: **every single
thing he can do, in one tab, tap it and it explains itself** — not nudges, not a one-time "did you know".

The lines that hold in every batch (unchanged): only his own data and public sources; no prediction about any person; no
private person searched for or profiled; nothing that can bill him; the brain never gets live GPS; the plain prompt under
5,500; no test skipped or weakened; the CI guard untouched; no model name in code, tests, docs or PR text; the Workshop
tab keeps its name.

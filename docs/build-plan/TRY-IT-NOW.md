# TRY IT NOW — the quick checks for what is live on your phone

Written 12 Nov for the five builds live since 11 Nov (pull requests 83 to 86). Each is two to five taps. The full phone checks stay in
OWNER-ISSUES (177, 180 to 183) and in each pull request; this page is the short version. Open ghosty.wvks62vknw.workers.dev. If a step does
not do what it says, tell me the number (for example "map 3").

## WHAT'S NEW (where every merge is listed)
1. Open the Workshop. The panel at the top says this week's merges in plain words. Tap GOT IT when read.

## ENGINE 3D (MAKE)
1. MAKE → the 3D studio → the bar's last key, 3-D ENGINE: a ground, a tray of shapes, one line saying every size is a first estimate.
2. One finger turns the view; two fingers pinch and slide.
3. Tap DESK, tap the ground: it stands there. Drag it. SIZE, then type 140 in W: it is 140 cm. Tap a swatch: it changes colour.
4. SAVE, leave, come back: still there. EXPORT: a .gltf file.
5. Ask "show me a room 4 by 3 with a desk", tap OPEN on the Stage: that room, in the engine.

## THE MAP (the third whole look)
1. Settings → Theme → THE MAP. Home is the plane in perspective, the rooms as districts, tonight's thing lit by the orb.
2. Tap a district: the room opens with the ruler along the bottom and the marker on that room.
3. Tap the kick over the ruler: the plan unfolds; tap a number.
4. Tap the field, type three letters: found; OPEN opens it.
5. Say "the map" in the chat from any other look: it comes back. Does it feel like a different app, not a dark theme?

## THE EYE (the fourth whole look)
1. Settings → Theme → THE EYE. Home is a wall of grain feeds, one sharp in the accent box, the globe turning faintly behind.
2. Tap a room feed (say LEARN): the room opens, the instruments run along the bottom with LEARN sharp.
3. Tap THE WALL · 7 FEEDS over the instruments: the plan unfolds; tap a number.
4. Type "rad" in the ask field: RADIO found; OPEN opens it.
5. Reduce Motion on (iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Motion): the globe stands still. Long-press a feed: the pin sheet.

## SPEAKER (your voice known, never a lock)
1. Settings → VOICE MODEL → GET on Wi-Fi (27 MB).
2. MY VOICE: enrol with three lines.
3. Say hello: the answer says YOU.
4. Hand the phone to someone and let them speak: "a voice I don't know", and it asks before it goes on.
5. Ask by voice for a send, a payment or an unlock: it still waits for your tap.

## SMALL FIXES 1
1. ONE INK on (Settings → Theme → ONE INK), Home, tap LOG: the sentences in hairline boxes, the arrow back.
2. With a book on the go: Home names it a few seconds after opening, with its capitals and apostrophes.
3. Settings → Theme → bigger text twice: every word whole.
4. The search key on Home: the jump bar comes up at once; the orb freezes behind it and moves again when you close it.

## DOCUMENTS (MAKE)
1. MAKE, Documents, NEW: type a few lines; make one a heading, one bold; add a link on a word.
2. PHOTO: add one of yours and make it LARGER; INSERT TABLE and fill a cell.
3. PAGE: choose Letter and add a footer line.
4. Export WORD and open it in Pages or Word (the same page, picture and table); export PDF.
5. Say "write a letter to my landlord about the damp" and tap OPEN: the same editor. Close the app and reopen Documents: the letter is listed.

## THE WEEKLY TRAINING (SYSTEM)
1. SYSTEM, then OFFLINE BRAIN TRAINING: the first card says when the last real run was, or "No real run has finished yet".
2. Tap CHECK FIRST: it reads only, and says all clear or the one step that stopped and what to do (the first time it will ask for the Kaggle and Hugging Face keys in SYSTEM, KEYS).
3. When it says all clear, tap RUN NOW: read what it will do (Kaggle's free GPU only, nothing can cost money), confirm.
4. Hours later the card shows the report card (old brain against new on the same questions) and Published or Not kept.
5. Published and you do not like it: tap ROLLBACK; the card says Rolled back.

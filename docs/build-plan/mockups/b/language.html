Rules

  • Hairline rules instead of cards; space does the grouping.
  • Serif only for titles and names; everything else is the sans.
  • Brass marks what is measured or active; vermilion only warns.
  • Tabs are words with Roman numerals, not icons.
  • Motion: slow, linear needle sweeps; reduce motion shows final values.

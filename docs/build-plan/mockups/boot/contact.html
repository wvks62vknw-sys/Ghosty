Reduce Motion on

The frames appear without motion: the reactor is simply lit, “GHOSTY · Online” and the five readings appear together, then the greeting, then Home fades in. No spin, no sweep, no slide; 1.2 s in all, and a tap still skips it. The warm return becomes a 150 ms brightening, no ring.

Sound

No sound file and nothing downloaded. The only sound is Ghosty saying the greeting, in his own voice, when voice is on in Settings.

Honest

Each line uses the last reading this phone holds (the same one each screen’s live line uses), so the boot never waits on the network. A reading it cannot get says so: Brain · can’t be reached right now, and the boot still ends at 3.4 s.

When it plays

The first open each day, and “reboot”. Never twice in a day otherwise; never over a notification you tapped (that opens straight to its screen).

What is real. The greeting (the same words Home’s title uses, from the time of day and the name in Settings), the reactor and MIND drawn from the mock-ups you said yes to, School starting at Nursery, today’s date, your city, the Home layout (date, title, weather, the hero’s four readings, Today, Roads near you, Gym) and the tab bar.

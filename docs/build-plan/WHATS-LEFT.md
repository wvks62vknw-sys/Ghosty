# WHAT'S LEFT — everything not yet on your phone, said plainly, 12 Nov

Four lists in the order things happen, then the questions only you can answer. QUEUE-11-NOV has the builders' detail; this page is yours.

## 1. Finished and checked, waiting to go live (seventeen; each goes up when its checks are green)
1. **The weekly training, for real.** Every week Ghosty's own small brain trains on what you taught it, on the free Kaggle machine, and the
   new brain reaches your phone only if it beats the old one; the whole path is now proved end to end, you see where it is, and RUN NOW
   runs it on your tap.
2. **Your phone's own hardware used properly.** 120 Hz motion everywhere, bigger "eyes" for the camera and the Lens on a Pro Max, the GYM
   using the phone's graphics chip, and the offline brain measured honestly for speed on your phone.
3. **Documents.** The second editor: a real Word-like writer in MAKE. Pages, bold and lists, pictures, tables, find and replace, Word and
   PDF out, someone else's Word file in, Ghosty writing into it.
4. **The readout ribbon and the orb that reacts.** A line that shows what Ghosty is doing; tilting the phone moves the light; the light
   follows the time of day; it says "On it." when you ask for something.
5. **Life easier.** "Hey Siri, ask Ghosty…", a Shortcut that files anything you share, the Brief read aloud when you first open the app,
   "What did I miss?", nudges from your bank statements, a photographed letter turned into a reminder and filed, watchers ("tell me when this
   price drops / this page changes / there's a strike on my line"), your email sorted into the Brief with draft replies for your OK.
6. **Polish 1, the edges.** Every screen says something when it is empty, loading, broken or offline; every error in Ghosty's words, never
   a browser's; every wait over half a second says what it is doing; the biggest text setting cuts nothing.
7. **Polish 2, the pictures.** The charts, maps, documents and 3-D rooms Ghosty shows you made good: labels, zoom, light, and a drawn
   picture can be sent to Telegram.
8. **Further ideas from October.** Interpreter mode (two-way spoken translation, offline), the orb as a map of what Ghosty knows about you,
   a lab tab for trying things, WALK ME THROUGH IT, WHAT CHANGED SINCE YESTERDAY, REHEARSE (practise a talk), the nature diary, and a Sunday
   note of what Ghosty learned that week.
9. **ORBIT.** The fifth whole look (a different app face, like THE MAP and THE EYE), where your Mind is the way in.
10. **GROWN UP.** The sixth whole look: today, with less on the screen.
11. **The Machine on the wall.** The Machine view on your TV or laptop screen, your phone as the remote.
12. **Spreadsheets.** The third editor: Excel's core in MAKE, with real formulas and .xlsx files in and out.
13. **Games 5.** A platformer, a cave-exploring game, and four board games good enough to beat you.
14. **The working engine.** In the car's 3-D view the engine is a real turning model: crank, pistons, valves, the four strokes named as they
    happen, every part tappable and named (first estimates, never certified).
15. **The LIFE room made right.** Your eight everyday packs (money, body, kitchen, people, work, creative, outside, home) given eighty fixes:
    every empty screen tells you the next thing, every wait is said, every number has its unit, START and STOP under your thumb, a ringing
    timer stops with one tap, amounts to the penny.
16. **Health.** A mark on everything Ghosty can do (WORKING, DEGRADED, NEEDS KEY, OFFLINE), so you never guess; when you ask it to set a
    reminder it checks the reminder is really there before it says Done; a PERMISSIONS page; crashes recorded instead of a blank screen; a
    THAT FELT OFF tap on any reply.
17. **Presentations.** The fourth editor: PowerPoint's core in MAKE. A report becomes slides, themes, notes, a slide show in your hand,
    .pptx out that opens in Keynote, someone's .pptx in.
18. **Home made right.** Home, THE BRIEF, the house line, the boot and the orb given twenty-three fixes: the brain's silence said once, no
    dashes, every fetch says what it waits for, a failed send says I COULDN'T with RETRY, the jump bar says "Nothing matches that." and
    offers the nearest room, READ IT TO ME stops on one tap.

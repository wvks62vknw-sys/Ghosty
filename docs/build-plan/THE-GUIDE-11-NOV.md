# The guide, 11 November: what is in Ghosty, how to find it, how to check it, how to show it; what is missing; what to add; what to polish

Written for your question of 11 Nov ("whats been added so far and give me a tutorial of how to find it, how to use it etc and how to like
see if it works and how to show it to other people; whats missing and hasn't been added; what could be added, even if i said something
like 2-3 weeks ago; what could be polished"). Plain words, one line a thing. Where a thing is still landing it says so.

## 0. Opening it, and getting the newest build

- Ghosty lives at your Worker's address (`ghosty.<your subdomain>.workers.dev`), added to the Home Screen. It opens like an app.
- A new build reaches your phone a few minutes after a PR merges to main and main's own run is green. To be sure you have it: close Ghosty
  fully (swipe it away), then open it. The boot film plays on every open; its last shot hands over to Home.
- If something looks half old, half new, close it once more. Your saved look stays (Settings, THE LOOK).

## 1. The house: how everything is found

Ghosty is one house with seven rooms. On every screen in THE SCAN the top line reads `THE HOUSE · 7 ROOMS` on the left and `YOU ARE IN <ROOM>`
on the right. Tap either, or hold the eye, and the plan unfolds: the seven rooms, your favourites starred, your recents clocked, what is in the
works dotted amber. The rule every screen is built to: anything you used lately is two taps away; anything else is three taps or one word.

| Room | What is in it | The way in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOME (Ask) | The orb, the greeting, the one thing of the hour, RECENT and FAVOURITES, the ask line | Where it opens |
| SEE | God's Eye (the globe with everything moving, MACHINE VIEW), the Lens, the Eye card, threats | The plan, or say "god's eye", "lens" |
| MAKE | Websites and apps, the Workshop, the Studio (floor plans, 3-D, the car), the Workbench and wind tunnel, the Converter, Documents | The plan, or "make a website…" |
| LEARN | Courses, MARK MY WORK, the ARCADE, the GYM, SCHOOL, the Library and its reader | The plan, or "teach me…", "arcade", "library" |
| PLAN | The Brief, the Agenda, SPENDING, the markets, alerts, the radio | The plan, or "brief", "agenda" |
| LIFE | Money, body, kitchen, people, work, creative, outside, home | The plan, or the pack's name |
| ME | Memory, Settings, System, Links, Social, Father and the origin, Every screen | The plan, or "settings" |

- **The ask line** at the bottom is also the jump bar: type a room's or a screen's name and it goes there; type a question and he answers.
- **Favourites:** hold any screen's card in the plan or on Every screen to star it. **Every screen** (Me, then Every screen) is the whole inventory
  as cards, each with what it does for you and a line to try.
- **WHY** under every reply says what ran and why. "Why did you do that?" works in chat and on Telegram.

## 2. What has been added since the redesign (8 to 11 Nov), one by one

Each: what it is, where, how to use it, how to see it is working, how to show someone.

### a. THE SCAN: the home world, and the boot as a film (on main 8 to 9 Nov)
- Where: the default look. Settings, THE LOOK, THE SCAN.
- Use: open Ghosty. The film plays (the world assembling, the scan building, the feeds coming up, the descent to your city), then Home with the
  room drawn around the orb and the house line on every screen.
- Working when: the film runs to the end without a stutter and Home knows where you are (the stamp line names your area and the hour).
- Show someone: close Ghosty and open it in front of them. That is the demo.

### b. THE THEME GALLERY: seven looks, three live (on main 9 to 10 Nov; THE MAP landing; THE EYE building)
- Where: Settings, THE LOOK. Seven pictures. A look is a whole different HUD, not a colour.
- Live now: THE SCAN, ONE INK (the whole app as a printed page: Home a masthead, the orb in ink, the rooms as a foot on every screen), GLASS
  (the look from before the Scan). Landing now: THE MAP (the field from above: your city's districts as the way in). Building now: THE EYE
  (attention is the material: the live feed as the ground). Not built: ORBIT, GROWN UP (their cards say their line and change nothing).
- Use: tap a card. Or say "one ink", "the scan", "glass" in the ask line. The colour chips below stay yours in every look.
- Working when: the whole app changes at once, every screen, with the same things still findable in two or three taps.
- Show someone: switch ONE INK on and hand them the phone; switch back by saying "the scan".

### c. THE LENS THAT JUST KNOWS (on main 8 Nov; its eyes offline since 10 Nov)
- Where: SEE, the Lens. One view, no mode keys on the picture.
- Use: point it. Text is read and, when it is not English, translated on the picture; codes open; objects are named; a plant, bird or insect
  named; a plane or a ship in the sky tagged; a meal totalled roughly; you are "It's you." once you have done ENROL ME; someone you introduced
  ("Ghosty, this is Sam") is named next time; a stranger gets no name and no guess.
- Offline: Settings, OFFLINE EYES, four packs (38 to 50 MB), each downloaded only on your tap on Wi-Fi with its size said. With flight mode on
  the finder, the words and the codes still work.
- Working when: the tags arrive within a second and sit on the thing, one box and one row per kind, and in flight mode words still read.
- Show someone: point it at a menu in another language, then at a plant.

### d. HIS VOICE KNOWN (on main 8 Nov; the real speaker model landing now)
- Where: say anything with the mic. The Me sheet says who was speaking. A courtesy, never a lock: your voice never opens anything on its own.
- Landing now: a real speaker model on the phone (26 MB in two parts) so "you" and "not you" is much surer. Introduce someone with them present
  and they are known next time.
- Working when: after a few turns the Me sheet names you as the speaker and a friend as "someone else".

### e. SHOW ME (on main 10 Nov; the brain's own picture 11 Nov)
- Where: in any reply with something to show. The first sentence lands at once; under it a Stage draws the picture the moment its data is ready;
  the sentence that points at it comes after.
- What is drawn: a place (the map), a route (the journey), numbers (a chart), a plan or a letter (the document), a formula (the simulation), a
  room or a part (3-D on the Stage). Since 11 Nov the brain can choose the picture itself; the phone's own reading is the fallback.
- Settings, THE LOOK, the SHOW ME row turns it off and on (on by default).
- Working when: "where is Lisbon?" gives a sentence, then the map under it, in under two seconds.
- Show someone: "show me the route to work", "how have my bills gone this year?" (with statements added), "draw me a 3 by 4 metre kitchen".

### f. SPEED (on main 10 and 11 Nov)
- What changed: the first screen's cost cut; the brain's start-of-turn reads side by side; calendars never on your turn; the model router
  decides faster; eleven lookups run together; replies stream from the first word, polished a sentence at a time when no tool ran.
- Working when: "hello" answers as it is spoken, word by word; two lookups asked together give one whole answer after the slower one, no double
  text. System, the SPEED card has the stopwatch numbers.

### g. OFFLINE MAPS (on main 10 Nov)
- Where: Settings, OFFLINE MAPS: the West Midlands, about 78 MB, on your tap on Wi-Fi.
- Working when: with flight mode on, God's Eye's MAP still draws Birmingham from the phone. Addresses, routes and ASK say they need a signal.
  Please say if the map is blank offline: it was built without an iPhone engine here.

### h. FIVE GAMES, AND NO CAP ON SKILL (on main 11 Nov)
- Where: LEARN, the ARCADE room: BLOCKS, BLAST, THE FLAP, THE CLIMB, THE BEAT, with DOOM, Quake, the DOS shelf, the five 3-D games of our own
  and chess with a real rating.
- Use: each is played by you and by Ghosty. His level has no ceiling: he keeps climbing. WATCH carries on by itself.
- Working when: his level number on a game's page is higher tomorrow than today.
- Show someone: THE BEAT or BLAST, then WATCH him play it.

### i. THE KERNEL (on main 11 Nov, your "yes to all")

## 4. How to check it works: the fifteen-minute walk

1. Close Ghosty fully and open it. The film, then Home with your area named. Say the time it took to the first screen if it felt slow.
2. Say "hello". The words arrive as they are spoken.
3. "Where is Lisbon?" The sentence, then the map under it.
4. Settings, THE LOOK: tap ONE INK, walk to LEARN and back, say "the scan".
5. SEE, the Lens: a page of text, a plant, your own face.
6. Flight mode on: the Lens's words still read; God's Eye's MAP still draws Birmingham. Flight mode off.
7. LEARN, ARCADE: play BLAST once; WATCH him play it.
8. "Show me the route to work" (Home and Work saved in Memory, ME, PLACES).
9. SEE, God's Eye, MACHINE VIEW: how fast the first line comes, how the drift feels.
10. Telegram: a text, a voice note, a forwarded message with a link.
11. Settings, THE LOOK, SHOW ME off: "where is Lisbon?" gives the sentence alone. Back on.
12. Anything wrong: a screenshot and the three words of what you tapped. That is all I need.

What "working" looks like: nothing says a browser's words ("undefined", "fetch failed"); nothing is a frozen nothing (a wait says what for);
everything is findable in two or three taps; every control can be tapped with a thumb.

## 5. How to show it to other people

- The two-minute demo, in order: open it (the film), "show me my city" (MACHINE VIEW), the Lens on a menu or a plant, "where is Lisbon?"
  (SHOW ME), ONE INK on, then "the scan". Let them hold it for the games.
- Say it is a web app you built, on your own Cloudflare account, free, with a brain that lives on the phone for the private things. The
  Deploy your own page (docs/DEPLOY-YOUR-OWN.md) is how a friend gets their own: a fork, half an hour, nothing of yours in it.
- Things not to show as a stranger's demo: the Lens on their face (it gives no name and no guess, by design, but it is their face); your
  Memory and Spending; your key. The guardian and the panic phrase are yours alone.
- Share the link to a website or an app he built: those are public pages and the proof that he makes real things.

## 8. What needs polish (where I think it is thin, honestly)

Your own words that still stand: "the tiniest bit of work on the UI everywhere", "small details", "the tabs inside tabs", "scroll issues",
"every single little thing looked over". Where I would put the polish, in order of how much it shows:
1. **The new worlds' edges.** ONE INK's chat log (fixing now), its empty and loading states, the way sheets open over the printed page; THE
   SCAN's Home under a long reply; the house line and the foot at the biggest text size; THE MAP and THE EYE will need the same pass once seen
   on the phone.
2. **The Stage.** SHOW ME's pictures are right but plain: the chart's labels, the map's zoom on a small place, the 3-D room's light, the
   hand-over from the sentence to the picture. A pass with your eye on each kind.
3. **The Lens's tags**: they can jitter between frames; one row can flicker as the namer and the finder disagree for a frame. A short smoothing.
4. **Words on screens.** Some screens still say too much (Settings, System, the Theme page's lines, the packs' cards). The rule is one sentence a
   screen; a sweep that cuts.
5. **Waits.** Anything over half a second should say what for, in his voice. The brain's first word is fast now; the Lens's first tag, a model's
   load, a pack's download and the Studio's first scene still show a blank beat.
6. **Error voice.** The browser's own words still hide behind some failures (the "operation was aborted" family). One funnel for every error
   into his own words, with a test that fails on a raw one.
7. **The boot film** on a slow open: when the first screen is late the film can hold on one shot; the hand-over should be smooth whatever the
   network does.
8. **The Agenda and System screens**: the only two never given their upgrade pass; they show it.
9. **Offline**: the map's first draw with no signal and the eyes' pack on a cold start, both only truly checkable on your phone.
10. **Telegram parity**: what he can show on the phone (the Stage) has no picture on Telegram; a drawn picture sent as an image would close it.

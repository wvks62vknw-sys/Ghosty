# The Library catalogue: Health & food (39 apps)

From the Atlas (Apps tab). Each is an app the owner wants. Give each the truest working core an archetype allows; one that needs its own build is listed in your report, never faked. The "like" names are only for you: never put a brand or app name in any text he sees.

## body.1 Workout Log
Log every gym set in seconds, see your personal bests climb, and get told when you've earned more weight.
Like (for you only): Strong, Hevy (with Fitbod-style progression)
- An exercise library of 300+ moves with the muscles each one works drawn on a body map
- Sets, reps and weight with last time's numbers shown inline, logged in two taps, fully offline
- A rest timer that starts itself with a tone (Safari on iPhone cannot vibrate)
- Personal bests and a chart per exercise: heaviest, most volume, estimated one-rep max as a first estimate
- Routines, supersets and templates; repeat last session in one tap
- Progression from his own history: add 2.5 kg once every set hit its target
- Plate calculator and warm-up sets worked out
- CSV export and import of Strong and Hevy files
Rule: Gym numbers stay on the phone; an estimated one-rep max is always labelled a first estimate.

## body.24 Food Log

Like (for you only): MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, MacroFactor

- His recipes become foods per serving
- Calorie and protein targets from his weight and activity, as first estimates
- Fibre, iron, vitamin D and other nutrients
- Re-log yesterday or a favourite meal in one tap
Rule: Targets are first estimates; the food table downloads on his tap on Wi-Fi with its size said; a plate photo is sent only on his tap.

# The Library catalogue: Travel & world (34 apps)

From the Atlas (Apps tab). Each is an app the owner wants. Give each the truest working core an archetype allows; one that needs its own build is listed in your report, never faked. The "like" names are only for you: never put a brand or app name in any text he sees.

## world.1 Navigate
Spoken turn-by-turn directions for driving, walking and cycling, with speed limits, cameras and rerouting, even on your offline map.
Like (for you only): Google Maps navigation, Waze
- Spoken turn-by-turn for drive, walk and cycle, with a big next-turn card
- Rerouting after a missed turn, asked from the next junction on the route
- Speed limit from OpenStreetMap and a gentle over-limit warning
- Safety-camera and road-report alerts with a countdown
- ETA and arrival time, with live traffic once your free TomTom key is in
- Keeps drawing on the offline map pack when the signal drops
- Night look and a dashboard HUD mirror mode
- Clean Air Zones and ULEZ flagged on the route
Rule: Your position stays on the phone and the brain never gets live GPS. The phone asks the public routing service itself, from a saved place, a 1 km square or the next junction. The screen must stay on, because a web app cannot run in the background.

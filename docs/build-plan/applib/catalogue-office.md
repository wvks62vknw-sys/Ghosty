# The Library catalogue: Office (37 apps)

From the Atlas (Apps tab). Each is an app the owner wants. Give each the truest working core an archetype allows; one that needs its own build is listed in your report, never faked. The "like" names are only for you: never put a brand or app name in any text he sees.

## office.26 PDF Studio
Open any PDF as real pages to read, mark up, rearrange, compress, protect and redact, all on your phone.
Like (for you only): Adobe Acrobat, PDF Expert
- True page view with thumbnails, outline and search
- Highlight, pen, notes, shapes and stamps saved into the file
- A page organiser: drag, rotate, delete, insert a blank or a scan
- Compress, with the size before and after
- Password-protect, and remove a password you know
- True redaction and hidden-metadata removal
- A text layer added to scans
- Compare two versions
Rule: Everything stays on the phone; it removes only a password you know.

## office.39 Mail
A beautiful inbox for your own email: read, search, snooze and summarise, with replies in your voice sent only on your tap.
Like (for you only): Superhuman, Spark, Gmail
- A threaded inbox
- A split inbox (people, newsletters, receipts) by rules on the phone
- Search by sender, words and attachment
- A one-line summary per thread and reply drafts in your voice
- Compose with formatting, signatures and attachments from Files
- Snooze and follow-up on the exact thread
- Unsubscribe through the message's own link, on your tap
- Ten seconds to undo a send
Rule: Your own mailbox only; every send is your tap; a message is read only when you open it.

## office.3 Proofread

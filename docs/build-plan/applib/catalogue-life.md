# The Library catalogue: Family & life (41 apps)

From the Atlas (Apps tab). Each is an app the owner wants. Give each the truest working core an archetype allows; one that needs its own build is listed in your report, never faked. The "like" names are only for you: never put a brand or app name in any text he sees.

## life.39 Keep in touch
Choose who you want to stay close to and how often, and Ghosty nudges you when it's been a while.
Like (for you only): Dex, Clay, UpHabit
- How often for each person (weekly, monthly…)
- A quick 'we spoke' log by tap or voice
- Things to bring up next time
- A this-week list on Home
- Call, message and FaceTime keys
- Birthdays and plans folded in
Rule: It uses only the cadence you set and the contacts you log. Nobody is scored or guessed about.

# The Library catalogue: Money & home (50 apps)

From the Atlas (Apps tab). Each is an app the owner wants. Give each the truest working core an archetype allows; one that needs its own build is listed in your report, never faked. The "like" names are only for you: never put a brand or app name in any text he sees.

## money.11 Receipts
Snap a receipt and it's read on your phone, matched to the payment and kept searchable for returns and tax.
Like (for you only): Expensify, Dext, Shoeboxed
- Camera capture with edges found and the page flattened
- Shop, date, items, VAT and total read on the phone
- Matched to the payment in his statement
- Category and tags, with a warranty or return window made from it
- Search by shop, item or amount
- Export as PDF, CSV or a zip of photos
- Works offline
Rule: Read on the phone; nothing is sent unless he asks.

## money.2 Envelope Budget
Give every pound a job from payday to payday, and move money between envelopes when life happens.
Like (for you only): YNAB, Goodbudget
- Money in, waiting to be given a job
- Envelopes with targets (monthly, or a sum by a date) and leftovers carrying over
- Move money between envelopes with a history of each move
- An overspent envelope turns red and asks which envelope covers it
- Checked against the latest statement so the envelopes match real money
- Works offline on the phone
Rule: Plain sums on his own statements, no forecast and no bank link.

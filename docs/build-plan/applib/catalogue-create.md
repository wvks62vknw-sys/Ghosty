# The Library catalogue: Creative (45 apps)

From the Atlas (Apps tab). Each is an app the owner wants. Give each the truest working core an archetype allows; one that needs its own build is listed in your report, never faked. The "like" names are only for you: never put a brand or app name in any text he sees.

## create.10 Script Cut
Edit talking videos and recordings by editing the words: delete a sentence and that part of the clip goes too, and the ums go in one tap.
Like (for you only): Descript, Opus Clip
- A transcript laid over the timeline, timed word by word
- Delete or move words to cut and reorder the video
- Ums, ahs, repeats and long pauses removed in one tap
- The best moments picked out as vertical shorts with captions
- Chapters and show notes drafted by Ghosty from the transcript
- Hands the edit back to VIDEO for finishing
Rule: Sound goes to your own brain only when you tap. Speakers are named only if they are you or people you introduced. Everyone else is 'Speaker 2'.

## create.15 Music Studio
Make full songs on your phone: record voice and instruments, play real-sounding instruments, add loops and effects, then mix.
Like (for you only): GarageBand, BandLab, FL Studio Mobile
- Unlimited tracks: audio recorded from the mic, and instrument tracks
- Free sampled instruments (piano, guitars, strings, drums), fetched when you tap
- A piano roll and a step editor
- A loop library of licence-clear free loops
- Effects: reverb, delay, EQ, compressor, pitch correction
- A mixer with automation, and latency calibration so recordings line up
- Smart chord strips and a drummer that follows the song
- Save as MP3 or WAV, as separate stems, or as MIDI
Rule: Instrument packs and loops download only when you tap, on Wi-Fi, with the size said.

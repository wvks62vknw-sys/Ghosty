## The big moves you are part of

- **One list and one morning page** (led by PLAN). You get one to-do list and one day-and-week timeline that every screen reads and changes, so there are no more separate copies. Each morning the Brief picks up a live line from every room, so one page tells you your whole day. Yours: `editors.c4`.
- **The camera as the front door** (led by LENS). Whatever you point the Lens at, it offers the next useful step: a receipt goes to Money, a poster to the diary, a book to the shelf, a letter into an editable document. The camera becomes the quickest way into every room. Yours: `editors.s3`.
- **Money and papers handled for you** (led by MONEY). Your statements, receipts, bills, warranties and household papers become one clear money picture, with a safe-to-spend number and a calendar of the days things are due. It works like a dedicated money app, using only your own records and plain sums, with no forecasting. Yours: `editors.c1`, `editors.s12`.
- **Studio-grade tools in every editor** (you LEAD it: make the pieces meet as one thing). Each editor gets the touches a specialist company would add: cells coloured by rules, comments, undo, a presenter view, photo edits you approve one by one, and a comic writer that follows your exact idea. Anything you make can then go live at its own link, always after your APPROVE. Yours: `editors.u2`, `editors.u3`, `editors.u1`, `editors.u7`, `editors.u5`, `editors.s6`.
- **Learning that adapts and talks back** (led by LEARN). Courses aim at the questions you got wrong, the shelf fills with free public-domain classics, and lessons can be heard, quizzed and argued with out loud. Learn becomes a personal tutor and a library in one. Yours: `editors.c3`.

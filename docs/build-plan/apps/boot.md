Read `docs/build-plan/APPS-ROUND.md` first. This file is your work, in order.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Files
Today every app saves somewhere different. One Files app for everything Ghosty keeps (documents, scans, comics, photos, recordings, models and exports), with folders, tags, search inside the words, a preview of any file, zips opened, a Recently deleted bin with undo, and a clean-up that says sizes. Every new app saves into it.

- **What it is:** One place for every document, sheet, deck, PDF and scan you keep in Ghosty, with folders, tags, search and sizes.
- **Match or beat:** Apple Files, Google Drive, Dropbox
- **Builds on:** Memory's 'find in my things', SYSTEM → STORAGE, Backup, every editor's store, CONVERT's zip
- **Effort:** M
- **Must-haves:**
  - Everything from every editor in folders and tags
  - Recents, favourites and search inside the contents
  - A preview of anything
  - Sizes and a clean-up of the biggest
  - Move, rename and duplicate
  - Import from the phone's Files and the Share sheet
  - Zip out, and a bin with restore

# THE APPS ROUND: SITES: Design

Read `docs/build-plan/APPS-ROUND.md` first. This file is your work, in order.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Design (Canva-class)
One canvas replaces the fixed Poster and Card, the birthday card and Logo Sketch. Every part moves by hand, with layers, snapping, hundreds of open-licence fonts, templates at every size and for all 14 occasions, photos with their backgrounds removed, a brand kit and mockups. The same canvas and brand kit then drive the Site Editor.

- **What it is:** Design anything from posters to invitations and social posts, with ready layouts, free fonts and photos, and every part yours to move.
- **Match or beat:** Canva, Adobe Express
- **Builds on:** Poster, Card and Logo (the creativeart layout code), Palette, PHOTO's cut-out, Fonts, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons (keyless public images), Sites and the social queue
- **Effort:** XL
- **Must-haves:**
  - A free canvas: move, resize, rotate, layer, align and group anything
  - Templates at every size: story, post, flyer, A4, invitation, menu, thumbnail
  - Open-licence fonts, icons and illustrations, plus free credited photos from Openverse and Wikimedia Commons
  - A brand kit: your colours, fonts and logos in one tap
  - Magic resize into every other size
  - Collage grids and photo frames
  - Invitations with an RSVP page on your sites, behind APPROVE
  - Print PDF with bleed, plus PNG, SVG, MP4 and GIF
- **His rules here:** Public images keep their credit. Downloads happen only when you tap. Posting and publishing each wait for APPROVE.

## 2. Then your existing apps, against their leaders (most important gap first)

- **Poster and Card, and the birthday card:** Move, resize and rotate any part by hand; Templates at every size and for all 14 occasions; Hundreds of open-licence fonts; His own photos with their backgrounds removed; An inside page, a back, and a print-ready folded PDF with bleed
- **Website builder and mini-apps:** A visual editor: tap a word, photo or section to change it; A template gallery with live previews; A section library to add, drag into order or hide; A brand kit applied to every page; Phone, tablet and desktop previews before APPROVE
- **Widgets and little games made by asking:** Seeing and editing the code beside a live preview; Versions to go back to; Installing as a mini-app, or putting it on his sites or the Arcade shelf; Duplicate and remix; A gallery with search, a sprite and sound library shared with the Game Studio, and scores kept
- **Published pages:** Cookie-free visitor counts; A full version history with compare and restore; Uptime checks with a push when a page goes down; Weekly speed, accessibility and SEO scores; A guided connect for his own domain
- **Social queue:** A calendar of drafts, each still needing its own APPROVE; Threads, multi-picture posts and a preview for each network; Cropping to each network's shape with the photo editor; A read-only inbox of replies, with drafted answers; Read-only like and repost counts from free APIs
- **Logo Sketch and Palette:** Many ideas at once, then refining one; Editing the logo itself; A brand board with mockups; A palette generator with locks and harmony rules; Contrast checks and saved palettes

## 3. Then new apps from your family, as many as stay whole and green

- **Site Editor** (L, like Framer and Squarespace (Wix Studio)): Edit your live sites like Squarespace: tap any word, photo or section to change it, drag sections, preview, then APPROVE.
- **Flows** (L, like Zapier and Make (n8n, IFTTT)): Automate anything Ghosty can do: when this happens, do that, built visually and run by your brain while your phone sleeps.
- **Ability Builder** (L, like Raycast extensions and Apple Shortcuts): Teach Ghosty a brand-new ability yourself: name it, give example phrases, chain steps and a card, and it becomes an app inside Ghosty.
- **Code** (L, like VS Code (github.dev) and Textastic; Replit mobile): A real code editor in your pocket: projects, syntax colours, run JavaScript, TypeScript and Python on the phone, with Ghosty pairing beside you.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Translate
The Interpreter shows up in three families with the same gaps. This adds good offline translation from Mozilla's free Bergamot packs, fetched on his tap on Wi-Fi with the size said, and an auto conversation mode with no key to hold. It also brings a DeepL-style typed screen with alternatives, phrasebooks per country, and translations drawn over signs and menus in the camera.

- **What it is:** Type, paste or speak to translate, with good offline packs for travel, alternatives, and a phrasebook you keep.
- **Match or beat:** Google Translate, DeepL, Apple Translate
- **Builds on:** DOCUMENTS' translate and letters, the INTERPRETER, the Lens's translation, the offline brain's rough translation
- **Effort:** L
- **Must-haves:**
  - A text box that finds the language itself and answers as you type
  - Offline packs (on-phone translation models per language pair) on his tap, size said
  - Alternatives, and formal or casual
  - Speak and listen buttons
  - Stars into a phrasebook sorted by trip
  - Conversation and camera modes one tap away
  - Letters changed between scripts
- **His rules here:** Packs download only on his tap, on Wi-Fi, with their size said.

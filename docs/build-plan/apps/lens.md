## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Photos
A private library behind the photo editor, filled from the photo picker: albums, favourites, and search by what is in a picture using the Lens's CLIP namer, which is already on the phone. People shows only him and the people he introduces, plus Places and On This Day. It also brings the editor's missing AI masks (sky, background, people), a live histogram, and on-phone denoise and super resolution fetched on his tap.

- **What it is:** Your photos in one private library that you fill from the photo picker, with albums, favourites, search by what's in them, and memories.
- **Match or beat:** Apple Photos, Google Photos
- **Builds on:** the PHOTO editor's shelf and store, the Lens's CLIP namer and object finder (search by what is in a photo), Faces (only you and people you introduce), Share to Ghosty, Backup
- **Effort:** L
- **Must-haves:**
  - Bring in photos and videos from the picker or the Share sheet, with the storage used said
  - Albums, favourites, hidden photos, and deleting with a 30-day Recently Deleted
  - Search by what is in a photo ('dog on a beach'), by date, and by text in the photo, all on the phone
  - People: only you and the people you have introduced, by the names you gave. Everyone else stays 'a person'
  - Duplicates and near-duplicates found, with the best shot suggested
  - Memories: a short film of a day or a trip with music, made when you tap
  - A map of where photos were taken, from the photos' own data, kept on the phone
  - One tap from any photo into PHOTO, Design or Photo Book
- **His rules here:** Faces are grouped only for you and the people you introduced. Strangers are never named. Where a photo was taken stays on the phone and never goes to the brain.

# THE APPS ROUND: COMIC: Paint

Read `docs/build-plan/APPS-ROUND.md` first. This file is your work, in order.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Paint
Ghosty has nowhere to draw at all. A pressure-aware brush engine with layers, blend modes, undo and time-lapse becomes the drawing core that the Comic maker's freehand inking, Drawing a day, the camera tracing aid and game sprites all reuse.

- **What it is:** A real painting app: pressure-sensitive brushes, layers and blend modes, and a time-lapse of every stroke.
- **Match or beat:** Procreate, Adobe Fresco, Infinite Painter
- **Builds on:** the PHOTO editor's graphics-chip tiles and layers, Palette, Coach's DRAW prompt, Convert
- **Effort:** XL
- **Must-haves:**
  - A brush engine on the graphics chip: pencil, ink, marker, watercolour, oils, airbrush
  - Pressure and tilt with an Apple Pencil on iPad; smoothing and a stabiliser for a finger on iPhone
  - Layers with blend modes, masks, alpha lock and a reference layer
  - Selection, transform, liquify and symmetry guides
  - QuickShape: wobbly lines snapped straight
  - Time-lapse replay saved as video
  - PSD, PNG and layered export
  - Colouring pages from open line art, or from your own photos turned into outlines

## 2. Then your existing apps, against their leaders (most important gap first)

- **Drawing a day:** A real canvas to draw on; Brushes, layers, colour and undo; Step-by-step drawing lessons; A gallery with time-lapse; A tracing aid through the camera
- **Comic maker:** Freehand drawing and inking by finger or Apple Pencil; A filter that turns his photo into inked comic art; Free panel shapes: angled gutters, broken borders, insets; More lettering: fonts, balloon styles, curved text, his own handwriting; A much bigger art set: poses, props, vehicles, backgrounds, weather
- **Comic reader and shelf:** Shelf search, sorting, collections, series and read marks; Page thumbnails and bookmarks; Smarter panel finding, and fixing a panel by hand; Page-turn animation, a night tint and auto-advance; Reading ComicInfo.xml when a comic is imported
- **Your week as a comic:** Photos, journal, places and mood in the story; Monthly and yearly editions; People he introduced in the cast, with looks he picks; Past weeks kept on a shelf every Sunday; Sharing as a short video

## 3. Then new apps from your family, as many as stay whole and green

- **Script Cut** (L, like Descript): Edit talking videos and recordings by editing the words: delete a sentence and that part of the clip goes too, and the ums go in one tap.
- **Music Studio** (XL, like GarageBand, BandLab, FL Studio Mobile): Make full songs on your phone: record voice and instruments, play real-sounding instruments, add loops and effects, then mix.
- **Podcasts** (M, like Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts, Overcast): Find and follow any podcast and download over Wi-Fi, with smart speed, voice boost, chapters and transcripts.
- **Voice Memos** (M, like Apple Voice Memos, Otter.ai, Just Press Record): Record ideas, lectures and meetings while Ghosty is open, then get a clean transcript, a summary and an archive you can search.
- **PhotoScan & Restore** (L, like Google PhotoScan, FilmLab, Remini): Rescue old prints and negatives: glare-free scans, negatives turned positive, scratches cleaned, faded colour brought back.

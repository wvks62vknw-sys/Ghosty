# THE APPS ROUND: POLISH3: The Arcade, console-class

Read `docs/build-plan/APPS-ROUND.md` first. This file is your work, in order.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### The Arcade, console-class
This turns the shelf into an Apple Arcade or Steam-class home. Each game gets a page with screenshots, controls and its licence. Continue Playing resumes from engine save states, and there are named save slots and a touch-layout editor with gyro aim. A Trophy Room keeps achievements, play time and bests for every game, including the ones he makes.

## 2. Then your existing apps, against their leaders (most important gap first)

- **The Arcade:** A proper page for each game: screenshots, controls, how to play, the licence; Continue Playing from engine save states, and named save slots; Achievements and stats for every game; Button remapping, a touch-layout editor and gyro aim
- **The Block World:** A survival mode with health, hunger, tools, crafting and an inventory; Animals and night monsters; More block kinds, light sources and switch-and-wire logic; Flowing water, falling sand, growing crops; More biomes, villages and dungeons
- **The Kart Racer:** Many more tracks, and mirror versions; Cups with points and a podium; Time trials against saved ghosts; Karts and drivers with stats and unlocks; A battle mode
- **The Platformer:** A world map with secret exits; Hidden collectibles that open bonus worlds; More power-ups; Boss levels; A course maker
- **The Cave:** Many more rooms and bosses; Movement abilities found over time; Characters, a story and side quests; Map pins and fast travel; Upgrades and charms
- **The Runner, Hopper, Flap and Climb:** Missions and a daily seeded run; Characters unlocked with coins earned in play; More power-ups and new worlds; A best-run card to share; Two players on one screen
- **Blocks, Blast and The Beat:** Solo modes for Blocks (Marathon, Sprint, Ultra, Zen) with modern rotation; Blast adventure levels and a daily puzzle; A level editor for The Beat; His own songs as levels; Practice mode with checkpoints
- **The Arena:** Teams, battle royale and snake modes; Skins and names; A bigger arena and more bots; Round history; Playing a friend's phone in the same room

## 3. Then new apps from your family, as many as stay whole and green

- **Clock** (M, like Apple Clock, Google Clock, Alarmy): Alarms, timers, stopwatch and world clock in one Clock, with alarms that still reach you when Ghosty is closed.
- **Calculator** (M, like PCalc, Apple Calculator (iOS 18), Calcbot 2): A beautiful scientific calculator with a history tape you can edit, live answers as you type, and units and currency inline.

# THE APPS ROUND: EDITORS: Documents, Sheets and Slides: pro edition

Read `docs/build-plan/APPS-ROUND.md` first. This file is your work, in order.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Documents, Sheets and Slides: pro edition
These are the editors he called out. Real typography and named styles, comments and his own tracked changes, colour-by-rule, pivots and a chart editor, free-form slides with snapping guides, presenter view, Morph transitions, and designer template galleries for all three. These are the Word, Excel and Keynote gaps that make the editors feel thin today.

## 2. Then your existing apps, against their leaders (most important gap first)

- **Documents:** Real typography: fonts, sizes, colour, spacing and named styles you can redefine; Comments, replies and his own tracked changes; Spelling and grammar checked on the phone, with accept or ignore; A designed template gallery (letter, CV, report, newsletter, invoice); A navigation pane that drags whole sections and folds headings away
- **Spreadsheets:** Colour-by-rule: colour scales, data bars, icon sets; Pivot tables and a one-tap summary by month or by shop; A chart editor with pie, scatter, area, stacked, combo and in-cell sparklines; Data validation: dropdowns, checkboxes and date pickers; Cell formatting: borders, merge, wrap, number formats and table styles
- **Presentations:** Free-form slides: any number of boxes, pictures and shapes, with guides, align, group and layers; Presenter view on the phone while the slide shows on the TV; A designer theme gallery and layout ideas for each slide; Magic Move or Morph and a full set of builds, carried to and from PowerPoint; Video, audio, GIFs and a soundtrack
- **Photo editor and shelf:** A real library behind the editor (albums, stars, flags, search by what is in a photo); AI masks: sky, background, people and their parts, tap an object; A live histogram with clipping warnings; On-phone AI denoise and 2x or 4x super resolution, fetched on his tap; Colour grading wheels, dehaze, clarity and texture
- **Video editor:** Styled, animated captions from the speech; Unlimited video, text and audio layers, with sound waveforms on the strip; A library of title animations, stickers and effects; Cutting out a person without a green screen, on the phone; Auto-reframe from 16:9 to 9:16, following the subject
- **PDF tools:** A true page viewer with thumbnails and pinch zoom; Annotations saved into the PDF; Filling forms and signing with a saved signature; A visual page organiser: drag, rotate, delete, insert, extract; OCR text layer so scans become searchable
- **The Workspace:** Toggles, columns, synced blocks and drag handles; Live embeds of his sheets, decks, PDFs, maps and whiteboards; A form view on databases; Database automations and buttons; A chart view of any database

## 3. Then new apps from your family, as many as stay whole and green

- **PDF Studio** (L, like Adobe Acrobat, PDF Expert): Open any PDF as real pages to read, mark up, rearrange, compress, protect and redact, all on your phone.
- **Mail** (L, like Superhuman, Spark, Gmail): A beautiful inbox for your own email: read, search, snooze and summarise, with replies in your voice sent only on your tap.
- **Notes** (M, like Apple Notes, Google Keep, Bear): Jot, tick, sketch and scan in one tap, and find any note instantly, kept on your phone like Apple Notes.
- **Proofread** (M, like Grammarly, LanguageTool, ProWritingAid): Your spelling, grammar and style checked on the phone in every editor, each fix shown for you to accept or ignore.
- **Scanner** (M, like Adobe Scan, Genius Scan, Microsoft Lens): Scan many pages in one go with edges found live, cleaned and turned into searchable PDFs or editable text.
- **Templates** (M, like Microsoft Create, Apple Pages templates, Canva templates): Hundreds of designer letters, reports, invoices, planners and decks that fill in your name, address and the date for you.
- **Day Planner** (M, like Sunsama, Structured, Motion): Each morning drag your tasks into today's hours around your events, then close the day with a two-minute review.
- **Read Later** (M, like Instapaper, Matter, Readwise Reader): Save any article from Safari in one tap and read it later clean, offline, highlighted, or read aloud on the commute.
- **Dictate** (M, like Wispr Flow, Dragon, Apple Dictation): Talk and your words appear typed and punctuated in any editor, transcribed on your phone with nothing sent away.

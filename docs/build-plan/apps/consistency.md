## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Ghosty App Kit: every app a real app
This is what makes 'everything that could be an app' look like one top company made it. It is one shared frame every app is built from: its own icon and name, a first run with sample data, search, undo, share and export, settings, a Home card, voice entry, one smooth scroll container and one calm place for notices. It fixes the scroll fights and the big white text popping up mid-screen at the root, so it has to land before hundreds of new apps.

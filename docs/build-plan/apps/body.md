## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Health Hub
It fills itself each day from a ready-made Shortcut instead of a full Health export, and finally shows what the iPhone and Watch measure: sleep stages, resting heart rate, HRV, VO2 max, workouts with routes, a smoothed weight trend, and months and years of charts. Highlights come from his own history and are never advice or forecasts. Steps, Sleep, Weight and My Health week all get better at once.

- **What it is:** Everything your iPhone and Watch already measure, from resting heart rate to VO2 max, on one dashboard that fills itself daily.
- **Match or beat:** Apple Health, Gyroscope, Bearable
- **Builds on:** My Health week's streaming export reader, the body trackers engine, the Siri shortcut links, Documents export (PDF)
- **Effort:** L
- **Must-haves:**
  - Every Health type in the export read: heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, VO2 max, active energy, exercise minutes, workouts with their GPX routes, weight, blood pressure, breathing rate
  - A ready-made Shortcut with a daily automation that sends only the day's totals (never routes), or copies them for a one-tap paste, so no full export is needed
  - Activity rings built from the export, and live steps from the motion sensors while Ghosty is open
  - Charts from a day to all time, with pinch to zoom
  - Plain highlights from his own history ('resting heart rate 3 lower than last month')
  - A one-page summary PDF for an appointment
  - Only new days read on each import
- **His rules here:** Totals only reach the brain, as today; highlights describe his past numbers, never forecast them; not medical advice.

- **My Health week, Steps, Sleep, Weight:** The day's totals arrive through a ready-made Shortcut, not a full export each time; Everything in the export: heart rate, HRV, VO2 max, sleep stages, workouts with routes; Months and years of trends, and a smoothed weight trend line; Activity rings, plus hour-by-hour and year views; Plain highlights from his own history, never advice and never a forecast date

## 3. Then new apps from your family, as many as stay whole and green

- **Workout Log** (L, like Strong, Hevy (with Fitbod-style progression)): Log every gym set in seconds, see your personal bests climb, and get told when you've earned more weight.
- **Food Log** (XL, like MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, MacroFactor): Log meals by barcode, photo, voice or recipe, and see calories, protein and 30 nutrients from open food tables kept on your phone.
- **Meds** (M, like Medisafe, Apple Health Medications): Never miss a tablet: reminders that reach a locked phone, a taken-or-skipped log, a refill countdown and a list for your GP.
- **Run Plans** (M, like Runna, Nike Run Club, NHS Couch to 5K): Couch to 5K up to a marathon: a week-by-week plan with guided intervals in your ear and runs ticked off automatically.
- **Form Coach** (L, like Kemtai, Tempo, Peloton Guide): Your camera watches each rep and tells you out loud to go deeper or straighten your back, scoring every set.
- **Birds** (XL, like Merlin Bird ID, eBird): Name a bird by its song, a photo or five quick questions on your phone, and keep a life list of everything you've seen.
- **Nature ID** (L, like Seek by iNaturalist, PlantNet, PictureThis): Live camera ID for plants, trees, insects and birds that works with no signal, plus seasonal challenges to find new species.

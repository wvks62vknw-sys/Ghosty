## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Game Studio 3D (with Code Blocks logic)
He asked for this by name. It builds on the 3-D Engine's three.js and Rapier physics: 3-D platformers, racers, physics puzzles and explorers, with Scratch-style logic blocks (when, if, do, with variables, timers and triggers) that also power the 2-D studio. It adds templates for each kind of game, imported art, particles, lights and camera zones, and one tap to play on the Arcade shelf or publish to his sites.

- **What it is:** Make real 3-D games on your phone, platformers, racers, physics puzzles and explorers, then play them, share them or put them on your sites.
- **Match or beat:** Roblox Studio, Unity and GDevelop 3D, with the ease of Nintendo's Game Builder Garage
- **Builds on:** The 3D Studio's 3-D engine pane (three.js scene, handles, undo, glTF export, free-models shelf), the vendored Rapier physics, the Game Studio's characters, quests, sound, SHARE and AS AN APP, the arcade kit's touch and gamepad input with saves and scores, and the brain's game tool
- **Effort:** XL
- **Must-haves:**
  - Six 3-D templates that play in one tap: third-person platformer, first-person explorer, kart track, physics puzzle, marble roll, top-down shooter
  - Rapier rigid bodies, joints and a character controller (walk, jump, slopes, moving platforms), with gravity, bounce and friction set per object
  - Building by touch: drag shapes and free CC0 models onto the stage, snap to a grid, sculpt and paint terrain, and set the sky, fog and lights
  - Logic without code: when-this-then-that blocks for triggers, doors, switches, enemies, timers, scores, and winning or losing
  - Instant PLAY from any spot, with touch sticks and controllers, and a ghost replay of the last run
  - Describe a game and Ghosty drafts it as marked changes you accept or reject
  - WebGPU where the phone has it and WebGL otherwise, with a frame-rate guard that lowers detail before a frame is dropped
  - Export as one installable page; it goes on your sites only after APPROVE
- **His rules here:** Free models and textures download only on your tap, on Wi-Fi, with the size said; publishing waits for APPROVE every time.

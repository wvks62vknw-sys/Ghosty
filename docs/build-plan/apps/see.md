## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Weather
A weather app of its own instead of a card in the Brief and a radar sheet. Next-hour rain, hourly graphs and 10 days for every saved place, Met Office warnings in plain words, radar played forward, air quality, pollen and UV, and pushes for home only. All from free sources that need no key.

- **What it is:** A full weather app: next hour's rain, a 10-day forecast, warnings and radar, with Ghosty's dry commentary and alerts to your phone.
- **Match or beat:** Apple Weather, CARROT Weather, Met Office
- **Builds on:** The Brief's weather, rain timing and what to wear, Rain radar, the air feed, CHARACTER dials, Reminders (the brain's push)
- **Effort:** L
- **Must-haves:**
  - Now, a next-hour rain line, an hourly graph (temperature, rain, wind, UV) and 10 days
  - Several saved places, plus 'here' asked by the phone directly
  - Official warnings (Met Office, MeteoAlarm) and pushed alerts for home
  - Radar back two hours and forecast rain ahead
  - Feels like, dew point, pressure trend, visibility, daylight gained, moon
  - Today against normal
  - A personality set by his CHARACTER dials
- **His rules here:** The phone asks the weather service for 'here' itself; the brain knows only saved places.

## 2. Then your existing apps, against their leaders (most important gap first)

- **Weather (Brief, rain radar, layers):** A weather screen of its own; A 10-day forecast and hourly graphs for temperature, rain, wind and UV; Every saved place and any city; Met Office warnings and pushes for home; Radar played forward an hour or two, plus lightning and satellite cloud
- **Map sheet, 3D map and offline maps:** Spoken turn-by-turn directions with rerouting; Place cards with opening hours, phone, website, access and photos; Saved lists of places, exportable as GPX or KML; Any region offline, with offline search and directions; Topo footpaths, elevation profiles and track recording
- **God's Eye globe:** A time slider that replays flights, quakes, radar and storms; Real 3D terrain from free elevation tiles; Today's clouds from NASA GIBS, with the day-night line; Historical imagery through Esri Wayback; Guided tours
- **Journey planner, Getting there, Live buses:** Live departures and vehicles inside the plan; GO mode with a get-off alert while riding, screen on; Disruption pushes for saved journeys; Tap a stop for live countdowns, and National Rail trains added; A 'leave now' push checked for delays
- **Road ahead:** Spoken turn-by-turn guidance; The speed limit from OpenStreetMap, with a gentle warning; Spoken camera and hazard alerts; An ETA that allows for traffic; National road reports if he adds the free key
- **Night sky (AIM) and Satellites:** A full-screen star map that works without the camera; Thousands of stars, constellations and Messier objects; A time slider and a red night mode; Every visible pass for 10 days, with sky charts; Search, with an arrow guiding him to the object
- **Live public cameras:** Scenic, beach and ski cameras; Live video where a source streams it; Day-long timelapses; Weather and sunrise beside each camera; Coverage beyond the nine regions
- **Nearby, Safer walk, Fuel and chargers:** Full place cards with opening hours and access; Filters for open now and step-free; Spoken walking guidance on lit streets; Live fuel prices for every station; Cheapest station along a route

## 3. Then new apps from your family, as many as stay whole and green

- **Navigate** (L, like Google Maps navigation, Waze): Spoken turn-by-turn directions for driving, walking and cycling, with speed limits, cameras and rerouting, even on your offline map.
- **News** (L, like Apple News, Google News, Ground News): All the news you choose, from hundreds of public outlets, in a clean reader, with the same story compared across sources.
- **Live trains** (M, like Realtime Trains, National Rail Enquiries, Trainline): Live departure boards, platforms, delays and calling points for any station, with your commute watched and alerts pushed to you.
- **Trips** (L, like Wanderlog, TripIt, Google Travel): Plan a whole trip on one map: days, places, bookings, weather and budget, with a countdown on Home.
- **Tickets & passes** (M, like Apple Wallet, TripIt): Your boarding passes and tickets in one offline wallet, barcodes redrawn crisp, the next one always on top.
- **Offline atlas** (XL, like Organic Maps, OsmAnd, Google Maps offline): Download any country's map on Wi-Fi and search, browse and get directions with no signal at all.
- **Trails** (L, like Komoot, AllTrails, OS Maps): Plan walks, rides and hikes on the topo map with the climb, surface and sunset shown, then follow them offline.
- **Night sky** (L, like Star Walk 2, SkySafari, Stellarium): Hold up your phone to name every star, planet, constellation and satellite, travel through time, and find anything with an arrow.
- **Car logbook** (M, like Drivvo, Fuelly): MOT, tax, service and insurance dates with reminders, fill-ups and mpg, and what your car really costs a month.

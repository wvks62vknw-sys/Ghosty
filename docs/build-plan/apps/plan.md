## 2. Then your existing apps, against their leaders (most important gap first)

- **Agenda (calendar):** Day and week timelines showing free gaps, with drag to move; Editing an event in place; Any month or year; Colour-coded calendars to show or hide, including iCal feeds; His own repeating events with exceptions
- **To-do and reminders (Agenda and WORK):** One list across Agenda and WORK; Projects with sections, and tags; Subtasks, notes and attachments; Timed to-dos the brain pushes to a locked phone; Priorities P1 to P4, and repeating to-dos
- **Mail drafts and email line:** An inbox with full messages and threads from his own IMAP account; Search, archive and snooze; A compose screen with formatting, attachments, signatures, Cc and Bcc, for new mail as well as replies; One-line thread summaries and replies in his voice, with a tone choice; Follow-up by thread, and attachments saved to the Library
- **Shortcuts and Scheduled tasks:** A visual builder with a trigger and steps; Triggers beyond the clock: feeds, pages, webhooks, mail, sheet rows; Conditions and branches; A run history with each step's input and output; Results handed back to the Shortcuts app, and a template gallery
- **Focus timer:** A focus screen of its own; Each session linked to a task; Stats and streaks; Named presets; Ambient sound that quietens at the bell
- **THREATS:** Floods and river levels; A push for big events near his saved places; A timeline of what changed; Volcano, tsunami and wildfire feeds; A map inside THREATS
- **Radio and Headlines:** The song on air, and station logos; A globe of stations; Pause and rewind live radio; Hundreds of outlets and a clean reader; One story compared across sources

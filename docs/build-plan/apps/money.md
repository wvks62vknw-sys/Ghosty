# THE APPS ROUND: MONEY: Money, effortless

Read `docs/build-plan/APPS-ROUND.md` first. This file is your work, in order.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Money, effortless
With no bank feed, getting money in decides everything else. Drop many files at once and every big UK bank's CSV, OFX, QIF and PDF is read, with overlaps removed. Several accounts sit side by side, with moves between his own accounts spotted. Shop names come out clean with a page per shop, and the charts open up when tapped. Budgets, bills and goals all rest on this.

## 2. Then your existing apps, against their leaders (most important gap first)

- **Money:** Effortless import: many files at once, every big UK bank's CSV, OFX, QIF and PDF, overlaps removed; Several accounts with balances, and transfers between his own accounts spotted; Clean shop names with logos, and a page for each shop; Charts you tap into, any two periods compared, payday-to-payday months, a flow chart; Leftovers carried into next month, budgets for groups of categories, and a push when a budget runs hot
- **Bills and subscriptions:** Catching bills whose amount moves each month; One bills list joining HOUSEHOLD and MONEY; 'You may be overpaying' lines from public facts; A cancel flow checked against the next statement; Free trials, with a push before they start charging
- **The car (My car, car money, Car · fix it):** One Garage page; A fuel log with miles per gallon and cost per mile; Service history, with reminders by date or by mileage; MOT and tax pushes, advisories turned into to-dos, a recalls check; A photo of the parking spot and a compass arrow back to the car
- **Work desk and documents library:** A CV and letter form, filled section by section; Designed templates with a professionally set PDF; Tailoring to a pasted job advert; Home-life letters with a proper layout and his signature; Timesheet and shift rota pages
- **Job applications and Interview practice:** A board with cards dragged between stages; Saving a job from its advert link; The CV and letter used, attached to each application; Questions from the real advert, with STAR scoring; Camera practice notes worked out on the phone, his face only
- **Markets and currency:** Shares and funds from a keyless source, or his own free key; Holdings with cost, profit and a breakdown of what he holds; Price and rate alerts pushed by the brain; Candle charts you pinch and drag; Every world currency at ECB rates on one multi-row screen, kept for offline
- **Savings goals:** Saving rules ticked off when the statement shows the money moved; A line chart for each goal; Celebrations at milestones; Interest, and inflation from ONS figures; Goals linked to price watches
- **Shared bills and Split a fare:** Groups with running balances; Uneven and item-by-item splits; Fewest payments to settle up; Several currencies; One splitter instead of two
- **Price watches:** A price history chart; A target price with a push; Several shops side by side; Adding from a barcode or a link; Back-in-stock watching
- **Household, Home inventory, Warranties and papers:** Pages for the rent day, the house's address book and the services; A push the evening before bin day; A card per item with its photo, value, receipt and warranty; End dates read from scans, with pushes 30 and 7 days before; A claim pack PDF and recall checks
- **Energy and meter:** Gas and electricity with his tariff; Cheapest hours from Agile prices and carbon intensity; His tariff against the price cap; Smart meter data only if he adds his key; Appliance running costs
- **Take-home pay and Timesheet:** A pay page with sliders; Scotland's bands; Two jobs side by side; A start and stop timer and a weekly grid; Export to CSV, PDF or an invoice

## 3. Then new apps from your family, as many as stay whole and green

- **Receipts** (L, like Expensify, Dext, Shoeboxed): Snap a receipt and it's read on your phone, matched to the payment and kept searchable for returns and tax.
- **Envelope Budget** (L, like YNAB, Goodbudget): Give every pound a job from payday to payday, and move money between envelopes when life happens.
- **Invoices** (L, like FreeAgent, Xero, Invoice2go, Zoho Invoice): Branded invoices, numbered and chased politely, marked paid when the money shows up in your statement.
- **CV Studio** (L, like Kickresume, Rezi, Teal, Canva's CV templates): A CV designed like the best templates, tailored to each job ad with a match score, exported as a crisp PDF.
- **Post Room** (M, like Adobe Scan, Evernote Scannable): Scan every letter: it's read, filed, and turned into a reminder or a reply, so paper never piles up.
- **Better Deals** (M, like Uswitch, MoneySavingExpert Cheap Energy Club, Snoop): Every contract's end date beside public prices, so you know when you're overpaying and what to ask for.
- **Claim It** (M, like Resolver, DoNotPay): Refund and complaint letters built on UK consumer law, with every deadline tracked until your money is back.
- **DIY Planner** (L, like B&Q's and Wickes' project guides, HomeZada projects): Tell it the job and get steps, tools, a cut list and materials from your room's real sizes.

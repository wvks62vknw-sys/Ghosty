Read `docs/build-plan/APPS-ROUND.md` first. This file is your work, in order.

## 1. Your flagship: build it whole, at the level of the market leader

### Journal
ME → MEMORY is Ghosty's diary about him, and there is nowhere to write his own. This is a Day One-class journal: photos, voice, place, mood and weather filled in, prompts and gratitude, a calendar and On This Day. It is locked, kept on the phone, and searchable by meaning.

- **What it is:** Write, talk or snap your day into a private journal with photos, mood and place, locked with Face ID and kept on your phone.
- **Match or beat:** Day One, Apple Journal
- **Builds on:** Documents editor (rich text), Memory diary and timeline, Body → Mood, saved places (Memory → Places), meaning-search model, encrypted Backup, voice recording
- **Effort:** L
- **Must-haves:**
  - Rich entries with photos, voice notes turned into text, mood and weather
  - Several journals (personal, kids, travel) with their own colours
  - Calendar, map-of-saved-places and On This Day views across years
  - Prompts from a public list of journalling questions when the page is blank
  - Face ID lock through a passkey, plus encrypted backup
  - Search by words and by meaning on the phone
  - Export a year as a print-ready PDF book
  - Streak and a gentle push at the time you choose
- **His rules here:** Places come only from your saved places or a photo's own data, read on the phone. The brain never gets live GPS. Entries stay on the phone unless you ask Ghosty about one.
